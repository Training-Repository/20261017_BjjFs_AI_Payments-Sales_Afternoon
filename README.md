# M365 Copilot Training — Payments (Sales), Field (Bajaj Finance Limited)

Training materials for a Microsoft 365 Copilot enablement session for the Bajaj Finance Limited (BFL) **Payments (Sales) — Field** team, delivered by Technizer India under the **GenAI for Enterprise Teams** program.

The session covers:
- Prompt engineering: meta-prompting and the RTCFT framework.
- Copilot on your phone for merchant pitches, objection replies, follow-up messages and visit notes.
- Copilot in Excel on a merchant onboarding tracker.
- The flow from a raw data file to a leadership-ready deck.
- Copilot Notebooks, to work across more files than the chat attachment limit allows.
- Turning a document into a formatted PDF.

> All data in this repository is **synthetic** and safe to use in training. Merchant, FOS and Team Lead names are fictional. `Merchant_Offer_and_FAQ_Sheet.pdf` is a **training sample**. Its prices, waivers and SLAs are illustrative, so do not quote them to merchants.

## How to use this material

1. Start with `MetaPrompting_Guide_PaymentsSales.md` to learn the meta-prompt formula and RTCFT.
2. Work through `BFL_PaymentsSales_MetaPrompts_Participant_Guide.md`. Try Stages 1, 3, 4 and 6 on your **phone** in the M365 Copilot app.
3. Move to `BFL_PaymentsSales_CopilotExcel_Participant_Guide.md` on a laptop (requires a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence).
   - **Copilot Chat only?** Use `BFL_PaymentsSales_Alt_Copilot_Chat_Participant_Guide.md` instead.
4. Practise the 9-step flow in `Excel_To_PowerPoint.md`.
5. Use `Copilot_Notebooks_Guide.md` to bring everything into one Copilot Notebook, with no re-attaching and no 3-file limit.
6. Keep `copilot-pdf-playbook.md` for when a leave-behind or review pack needs to go out as a PDF.

## Requirements

- Microsoft 365 Copilot or Copilot Chat, with the **M365 Copilot app** or Teams on your phone.
- Access to Copilot Notebooks in the M365 Copilot app, for `Copilot_Notebooks_Guide.md`.
- The files uploaded to OneDrive, so they can be attached in Copilot Chat. BFL allows up to 3 attachments per prompt.
